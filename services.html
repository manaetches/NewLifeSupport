What We Offer

Our Services

Comprehensive, person-centred support tailored to your individual needs and goals.

🏠

Daily Living Support

Compassionate carers and support workers providing assistance with day-to-day living.

  • Home assistance
  • Community engagement
  • Respite care
  • Personal care support
💪

Physical Therapy

Expert physical rehabilitation to restore movement, function and confidence.

  • Rehabilitation programs
  • Mobility improvement
  • Functional recovery
  • Strength & conditioning
🔧

Occupational Therapy

Empowering independence through tailored assessments and practical solutions.

  • Functional assessments
  • Home modifications
  • Adaptive equipment guidance
  • Independence planning
📋

Support Coordination

Navigating complex systems so you can focus on living your best life.

  • Personalised planning
  • Advocacy & guidance
  • Wellbeing monitoring
  • Financial support navigation
Inclusivity

Who We Support

We support individuals of all ages and backgrounds across a wide range of conditions and challenges. Whether your needs are short-term or lifelong, we're here for every step of your journey.

🧠 Life-changing injuries
♿ Long-term disabilities
🧬 Neurological conditions (TBI, stroke, spinal injuries)
🧩 Cognitive or behavioural challenges
🦽 Mobility and functional limitations
⏱️ Short-term & long-term care needs
📝 ACC & private-funded clients
🌍 All ages · All backgrounds · All journeys

Funding Options

ACC Funded

We work with ACC-funded clients to ensure you receive the full support you're entitled to following an accident or injury.

Privately Funded

Flexible private funding arrangements tailored to your circumstances and support needs.

Self-Referred

You don't need a referral to get started. Contact us directly and we'll guide you through the process.
